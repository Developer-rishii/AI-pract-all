# Experiment 7: To solve water-jug puzzle using Depth First Search (DFS) algorithm.

## Theory:
A Water Jug Problems is stated as: You are given two jugs, a 4-gallon one and a 3-gallon one. Neither has any measuring markers on it. There is a pump that can be used to fill the jugs with water. How can you get exactly 2 gallons of water into the 4-gallon jug? 
Another instance of the problem is: 
You have three jugs, measuring 12 gallons, 8 gallons, and 3 gallons, and a water faucet. You can fill the jugs up or empty them out from one to another or onto the ground. You need to measure out exactly one gallon.
